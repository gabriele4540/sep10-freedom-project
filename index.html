Veterinary Medicines & Engineering

Technology helping animals live healthier lives.

Overview

Veterinary medicine and engineering work together to improve the health and safety of animals. Modern technology helps veterinarians detect illnesses, monitor pets, and create better treatments for animals.

Part A: Existing Technology

  • X-rays and ultrasounds detect broken bones and illnesses.
  • 3D printing creates medical tools and models.
  • Laser therapy helps treat pain and sickness.
  • AI collars can detect when pets may be sick.
  • PetPace devices can warn owners about seizures.
  • Advanced imaging helps veterinarians study tumors safely.
  • Artificial intelligence helps determine pet health conditions.
  • Wearable trackers help find lost pets.
  • E-collars help train dogs safely.

These technologies help pets recover faster and stay healthy. Some devices can even predict health problems before they become serious.

Part B: Possible Future Technology

The Trick Medicine Treat

  • A crunchy medicine treat that helps dogs take medication.
  • Helps improve skin irritation and fur health.
  • Comes in different flavors for picky pets.
  • Does not need refrigeration.
  • Made without toxic artificial flavors or added sugars.
  • Can be used 2–3 times daily with meals.
  • Designed only for dogs and not safe for humans.

This future technology could help pets recover more comfortably while making medicine easier for pet owners.
