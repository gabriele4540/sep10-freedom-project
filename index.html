Veterinary Engineering

Veterinary Medicines and Engineering

Overview/Introduction/Context

Part A: Existing Technology

  • X-rays and ultrasounds detect broken bones and illness.
  • 3D printing helps create medical tools and devices.
  • Laser therapy helps treat animal sickness.
  • AI collars can detect whether pets are sick.
  • PetPace devices can predict seizures in pets.
  • Advanced imaging helps veterinarians examine tumors.
  • AI helps determine pet health conditions.
  • Wearable trackers help locate lost pets.

These technologies help veterinarians keep pets healthy and safe.

Part B: Possible Future Technology

  • The Trick Medicine Treat helps dogs take medicine easier.
  • The treat improves skin and fur health.
  • It comes in multiple flavors for picky pets.
  • The treat does not require refrigeration.
  • It is designed only for dogs.

This future invention could help pets recover while enjoying a tasty treat.
