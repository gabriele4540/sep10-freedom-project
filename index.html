Veterinary Technology

Veterinary Medicines and Engineering

This project explains how technology helps veterinarians care for animals. It also explores possible future inventions that could improve pet health and medicine.

Part A: Existing Technology

  • X-rays and ultrasounds help detect broken bones and internal problems in animals.
  • 3D printing can create models and medical tools for veterinary care.
  • Laser therapy is used to help treat illnesses and reduce pain in animals.
  • AI collars can help detect whether your pet may be sick.
  • PetPace collars are designed to monitor pets and detect possible future seizures.
  • Advanced imaging technology helps veterinarians zoom in on tumors and other health problems for safer treatment.
  • Artificial intelligence helps veterinarians determine the health condition of pets.
  • Wearable tracking devices help owners locate lost pets.
  • E-collars can help train dogs not to eat harmful objects or perform dangerous behaviors.

The technologies listed above help veterinarians keep animals healthy and safe. Some technologies, like AI-powered collars, can detect illnesses early and even warn owners about possible seizures before they happen.

Part B: Possible Future Technology

The Trick Medicine Treat

  • Many pets refuse to take medicine because they dislike the taste. The Trick Medicine Treat would make medicine taste better while helping pets heal.
  • Unlike hiding medicine in peanut butter, this treat would include ingredients designed specifically to improve healing.
  • The treat could help with skin irritation and improve a dog's fur health.
  • For example, my dog has dermatitis, which causes itchy skin and dark spots. Current medicines help a little, but there is no permanent solution yet.
  • The treat would come in multiple flavors so pets can enjoy taking their medicine.
  • The medicine would not need refrigeration.
  • The treat would be a crunchy gummy cluster without toxic artificial flavors or added sugars.
  • It could be used two to three times a day with meals to help dogs recover.
  • This medicine would only be for dogs and should not be consumed by humans.
